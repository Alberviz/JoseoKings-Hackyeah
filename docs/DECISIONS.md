# Decisions

Short log of technical decisions the whole team must respect. Newest first.
Only Alberto (with Claude) adds entries. If you disagree, say so in a PR comment.

| Date       | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Why                                                                                                                                                    |
| :--------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2026-10-03 | The app stops at beta: one complete loop (setup, child home, check-in, mission, reward, parent screens, report). Scope frozen to the list in `docs/HANDOFF.md` section 2b; after it, we pause and decide.                                                                                                    | Alberto wants a stable beta before adding anything, with fewer moving parts and a demo that works.                                                     |
| 2026-10-03 | Core check-in questions (proposed by Álvaro): belly comfort, energy, play pace. Three options each, 0 is the easiest day and 2 the hardest. The bathroom question is not asked to the child; parents can log it. Ids and scale in `src/config/content-ids.ts`. Sources still to verify by Farouk and Álvaro. | The biomedical team chose child-friendly wording and left out the embarrassing clinical items. One direction for every question keeps the code simple. |
| 2026-10-03 | New product: a family app for children aged 8 to 12 with IBD (check-in on drawings, companion, gentle movement missions, parent mode, doctor report). Replaces the restroom map and the menu reader, which are paused. Spec: `docs/PRODUCT.md`.                                                              | The team chose to work on communication between children and parents and with the doctor, in category Sport & Healthcare.                              |
| 2026-10-03 | One device with two modes (child, and parent behind a PIN). No backend, no accounts, no sync. QR from child to parent device is a stretch goal.                                                                                                                                                              | Keeps health data on the device and the demo reliable.                                                                                                 |
| 2026-10-03 | Activity is never measured. Missions are confirmed socially (alone, with someone, with family by PIN) and every record carries a neutral label. Sensors and camera are roadmap.                                                                                                                              | A PWA cannot read watches, and policing a child with sensors is fragile and invasive.                                                                  |
| 2026-10-03 | Rewards depend on checking in, never on the answer or the mission kind. A separate team track rewards missions done with someone. No punishment, no streak that breaks.                                                                                                                                      | Protects honest answers and avoids guilt in a child with a chronic illness.                                                                            |
| 2026-10-03 | No impact exercise or jumping in v1. Missions are gentle: breathing, stretching, balance, wall sit. The child picks only from missions the parents enabled.                                                                                                                                                  | Whether a child can do impact exercise is a medical decision. The app does not assign exercise by symptom.                                             |
| 2026-10-03 | No predictions, no energy percentage, no "bone shield" or any index. The energy answer is what the child marked. Food entries show co-occurrence counts only.                                                                                                                                                | Avoids medical claims and possible medical-device territory. Unverified; to be checked before the pitch.                                               |
| 2026-10-03 | The report is built on the device and printed or saved as PDF with `window.print()` and print CSS. No PDF library. Charts are small SVG components; the companion is SVG with CSS animation.                                                                                                                 | Smallest and most reliable option for 20 hours.                                                                                                        |
| 2026-10-03 | Parent PIN: 4 digits, salted PBKDF2 hash, lock after 5 tries, auto-lock after 90 seconds. It is a separation between modes, not security.                                                                                                                                                                    | Honest framing: localStorage is readable by anyone with the device.                                                                                    |
| 2026-10-03 | `zod` is approved as a dependency (task T1).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Validation of everything read from `localStorage`.                                                                                                     |
| 2026-10-03 | Roles and owners are assigned in `docs/TASKS.md`; Claude keeps the repo organisation; every AI starts from `AGENTS.md` and the prompt in `docs/TEAM.md`.                                                                                                                                                     | One flow for all tools and people.                                                                                                                     |
| 2026-10-03 | `main` is protected (PR, green CI, code owner approval, linear history, no force-push). Admins may push directly, so Alberto and Claude can push hotfixes. Teammates never push to `main`.                                                                                                                   | Fast hotfixes during the hackathon without losing review for normal work.                                                                              |
| 2026-10-03 | Styling with styled-components in a sibling `.style.ts` file. `.tsx` files only compose components; ESLint blocks raw HTML tags, `style` and `className`.                                                                                                                                                    | Readable components, one place for styles, rule enforced automatically for every AI agent.                                                             |
| 2026-10-03 | Pre-commit "prelint" step with husky + lint-staged, instead of the `prelint` npm package.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | The `prelint` package (2022) only checks `.js` files and uses ESLint 4; it would ignore every `.tsx`.                                                  |
| 2026-10-03 | **Superseded:** Vision API for the menu reader: Gemini.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | The team already uses Gemini; one structured JSON call per photo.                                                                                      |
| 2026-10-03 | **Superseded** by the new product above: Scope: two pillars (urgent restroom map, menu reader) plus a local profile.                                                                                                                                                                                         | Five modules do not fit in 24 hours. See out-of-scope list in `docs/TASKS.md`.                                                                         |
| 2026-10-03 | Health data stays on the device. No accounts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | GDPR: health data is special-category data. Simplest compliant design.                                                                                 |
| 2026-10-03 | PWA with Serwist (`@serwist/turbopack`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Official option recommended by the Next.js 16 docs for Turbopack.                                                                                      |
