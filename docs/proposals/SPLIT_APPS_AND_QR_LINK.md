## 1. Why split the app

The current product is "one device, two modes". The critique that hurts most in a pitch is the **shared-device fallacy**: if the phone is the parents', the parent hands it over saying "do your check-in" (the interrogation is back); if the phone is the child's, the parent types a PIN on the child's phone (it feels like surveillance). A second one is **data loss and sync**: one phone, one `localStorage`, months of data two days before the consultation.

Two installable apps on one domain answer both without a server:

- The **child app** is the child's. It works alone from day one (own setup, all missions enabled by default). Nobody opens it with a PIN.
- The **parent app** is the parents'. It has the PIN, the daily log, the diet, the rewards, the report.
- The apps talk **only through QR codes scanned at home**, encrypted, with no server, no account and no network. The child decides when to share ("Show my code to my parents"); the parent decides what to send (missions enabled, rewards from home).
- A **web home** at `/` explains the why and the what, and has the two install buttons. Judges land there.

What it keeps from `PRODUCT.md`: health data on the device, no medical advice, reward the act, no punishment, confidence labels, demo data labelled.

Implementation notes:

- `AppStateProvider` takes `storageKey` and `appRole` and moves from `AppProviders` into the two segment layouts. `loadState / saveState / clearStorage` take the key. First load of a new key migrates the legacy `crohncare_app_state` if present.
- `AppState` v2 adds (all optional for old data): `appRole`, `family { familyId, keyB64, pairedAt } | null`, `specialRewards[]`, `rewardClaims[]`, `lastShareDate`, `lastImportAt`. The economy fields (`fire`, `coins`, inventory) are V1's; this proposal reuses them, it does not define its own.
- Root `src/app/manifest.ts` is removed (the home never claims a scope). Tinted icon variants per app.
- `src/lib/link/`: pure TypeScript, unit-tested. `encodePairing / decodePairing`, `encodeShare / decodeShare` (compact arrays, `deflate-raw` when available, base64url, prefixes `CCP1:` and `CCD1:`), `splitFrames / joinFrames`, `mergeShare(parentState, payload)` (upsert check-ins by date, logs and claims by id, never deletes). AAD = familyId, so a code from another family fails clearly.
- `src/components/features/link/`: `QrDisplay`, `QrScanner` (camera, photo, paste), child `ShareScreen`, parent `FamilyLinkScreen`.

```
Parent app                                   Child app
  setup: familyId + random AES-GCM key
  pairing QR (plain, no health data) ------>  stores family, applies missions and rewards
  <------ data QR (encrypted, 1..n frames)    check-ins, mission logs, reward claims, chosen range
  decrypt, merge, "Updated from Lucas's app"
  re-show pairing QR after changes -------->
```

## 4. Economy: one proposal that fits the sketch, the team's document and `PRODUCT.md` 5.2

The team's document says: **coins** come from playing and buy food and accessories; the **fire bar** (0 to 100) rises when the dragon eats; **real-life plans set by the parents are bought with fire**, not coins. `V2-CHILD-PLAN.md` says the same, and also "fire only goes up", which contradicts spending it. Proposal:

- **Coins**: earned by the act (check-in, including "not today"; mission, including rest; chest after play). Never depend on the answer. Spent by the child on food and accessories. Never below zero.
- **Fire**: rises only when the child feeds the dragon. It never decays and the app never lowers it. The only time it goes down is when **the child chooses** to light a reward from home ("the dragon breathes fire to light the celebration"). That is spending, not punishment. If Alberto prefers "fire never goes down" literally, the alternative is: when fire reaches 100 the dragon earns one **flame token** and the bar starts a new cycle; rewards from home cost flame tokens.
- **Rewards from home** (`specialRewards`): created by the parents (name + price), travel to the child in the pairing QR, appear in the shop as "From home". A claim is a **request, not a promise**: the child's app says "Ask your parents when", the claim travels back in the data QR, the parents see "Claimed: Choose today's dinner (Oct 3)" and mark it done when it happens. This removes the "karting on a Tuesday" friction.
- A child in a flare still earns: "I don't feel like it today" and a rest mission give coins. Nothing requires moving.

Why this is compatible with `PRODUCT.md` 5.2 "parents never give rewards": the companion's progress (items, colours, badges) is still the child's and parents cannot touch it. Rewards from home are a separate, optional layer that the family defines together, with the same rule: the price never depends on what the child answered. `PRODUCT.md` needs a one-paragraph update if Alberto agrees (decision 3 below).

## 8. Decisions for Alberto

1. **Split into two installable apps with a QR link** (this proposal), or keep one device and two modes for the hackathon and put the link on the roadmap slide.
2. **Fire spending**: fire goes down only when the child lights a reward from home, or fire never goes down and rewards cost flame tokens earned each time the bar fills.
3. **Rewards from home**: approve the paragraph for `PRODUCT.md` 5.2 (claims are requests the parents confirm; prices never depend on answers).
4. **Dependencies**: `qrcode` and `jsqr`.
5. **Medication**: keep "no names" with the daily / periodic split, or allow parent-defined labels (the team's document asks for a list with ticks).
6. **Bathroom data**: the simple block with "don't know", or Bristol as the team's document asks.
7. **Owner of V7**: fold it into L5 (special rewards screen) or keep it with Fable.
