Hi Claudia, here is the verified list of clinical sources and references for T18 (submission PDF, README, sources list), based on PR #38:

1. Clinical Instruments & Question Sources:
- Belly comfort: Pediatric Crohn's Disease Activity Index (PCDAI) - Hyams JS, Ferry GD, Mandel FS, et al. Development and validation of a pediatric Crohn's disease activity index. J Pediatr Gastroenterol Nutr. 1991;12(4):439-447. Combined with visual self-report principles from Faces Pain Scale - Revised (FPS-R) - Hicks CL, von Baeyer CL, Spafford PA, et al. Pain. 2001;93(2):173-183.
- Energy: PCDAI General Well-Being item (Hyams et al., 1991) and PedsQL Multidimensional Fatigue Scale child self-report - Varni JW, Burwinkle TM, Katz ER, et al. Cancer. 2002;94(7):2090-2106.
- Play pace / Physical activity: IMPACT-III Pediatric IBD Health-Related Quality of Life Questionnaire - Otley A, Smith C, Nicholas D, et al. J Pediatr Gastroenterol Nutr. 2002;35(4):557-563.

2. Physical Activity & Relaxation Mission Sources:
- Dragon Breathing: Diaphragmatic breathing and guided relaxation for pediatric abdominal pain and IBD stress reduction (Szigethy et al., J Am Acad Child Adolesc Psychiatry 2007; van Tilburg et al., Cochrane Database Syst Rev 2009).
- Movement safety adaptations for pediatric IBD: Safe flexibility, avoiding Valsalva maneuver during isometric loading (preventing intra-abdominal pressure spikes and urgency), joint-sparing balance for enteropathic arthralgia risk.
